# Case Summary: Digital Account Opening for a Canadian Bank

Document ID: C01
Type: Case summary
Owner: Banking & Financial Services, Canada
Last updated: 2025-12-02
Status: Current

- Problem: account opening took 45 minutes in branch; online drop-off was 60%.
- Solution: mobile-first onboarding with digital identity verification.
- Result: online completion rose from 40% to 71% in 6 months.
- Lesson: involve compliance from week 1; late KYC changes cost 3 weeks.
