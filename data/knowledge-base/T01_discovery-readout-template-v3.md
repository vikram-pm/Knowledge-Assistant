# Discovery Readout Template v3

Document ID: T01
Type: Template guide
Owner: Delivery Excellence, Canada
Last updated: 2026-02-05
Status: Current

## Required sections
1. Executive summary (one slide)
2. What we heard: themes from interviews
3. Current-state journey and pain points
4. Target-state options with pros and cons
5. Recommended roadmap and high-level estimate
6. Risks, assumptions and next steps

Keep the main deck to 20 slides or fewer. Put detail in an appendix.
