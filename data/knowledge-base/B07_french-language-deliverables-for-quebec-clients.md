# French-Language Deliverables for Québec Clients

Document ID: B07
Type: Playbook
Owner: Delivery Excellence, Canada
Last updated: 2026-03-20
Status: Current

- Under Québec's Charter of the French Language, as amended by Bill 96, client-facing deliverables and software interfaces for Québec clients must be available in French.
- Plan translation time in the schedule: allow 5 business days per major deliverable.
- Use approved translation vendors; do not rely on unreviewed machine translation for final deliverables.
- Confirm language requirements with the client at kickoff.
