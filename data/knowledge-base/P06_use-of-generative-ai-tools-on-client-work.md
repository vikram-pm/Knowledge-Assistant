# Use of Generative AI Tools on Client Work

Document ID: P06
Type: Policy
Owner: Office of AI Governance
Last updated: 2026-06-01
Status: Current

## Approved tools
Only enterprise-approved AI tools listed on the AI Governance intranet page may be used for client work.

## Rules
- Never paste client confidential data or personal information into public AI tools.
- AI-generated content in client deliverables must be reviewed by a person before it is sent.
- Disclose AI assistance to the client when the SOW or client policy requires it.
- Log new AI use cases with the Office of AI Governance before a pilot starts.
