# Statement of Work Guide

Document ID: T03
Type: Template guide
Owner: Legal and Delivery Excellence, Canada
Last updated: 2025-11-30
Status: Current

Every SOW must include: scope and out-of-scope items, deliverables and acceptance criteria, timeline and milestones, commercial model (fixed price or time and materials), roles and responsibilities, data residency terms, assumptions and dependencies, and the change request process (see P08).
