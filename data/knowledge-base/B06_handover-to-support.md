# Handover to Support

Document ID: B06
Type: Playbook
Owner: Application Services
Last updated: 2024-06-20
Status: Current

- Start handover planning 6 weeks before go-live.
- Provide runbooks, architecture diagrams and known-issue lists to the support team.
- Standard warranty (hypercare) period after go-live: 30 days, unless the SOW states otherwise.
- Support team signs the handover acceptance form before the project team rolls off.
