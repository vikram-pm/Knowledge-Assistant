# Client Confidentiality and NDA Handling

Document ID: P05
Type: Policy
Owner: Legal, Canada
Last updated: 2025-11-05
Status: Current

- Every team member signs the client-specific NDA before accessing client systems or documents.
- Signed NDAs are stored in the engagement's legal folder, not on personal drives.
- Client names may not be used in internal case studies without written client consent. Use anonymised labels such as "a Canadian bank".
- Report a suspected confidentiality breach to Legal within 24 hours.
