# Discovery Phase Playbook

Document ID: B01
Type: Playbook
Owner: Delivery Excellence, Canada
Last updated: 2026-02-05
Status: Current

## Duration
Typically 3–6 weeks.

## Activities
1. Stakeholder interviews (8–15 people across business and IT).
2. Current-state process mapping.
3. Pain point and opportunity prioritisation.
4. Target-state options with high-level estimates.
5. Discovery readout to the client sponsor.

## Outputs
Discovery readout (use template T01), prioritised backlog, high-level roadmap and estimate.
