# Case Summary: Loyalty Platform for a National Retailer

Document ID: C05
Type: Case summary
Owner: Retail, Canada
Last updated: 2026-01-28
Status: Current

- Problem: three separate loyalty programs after acquisitions.
- Solution: unified loyalty platform with a single member ID.
- Result: 2.1 million members migrated; active members up 18% in a year.
- Lesson: Québec launch needed French content ready 3 weeks earlier than planned.
