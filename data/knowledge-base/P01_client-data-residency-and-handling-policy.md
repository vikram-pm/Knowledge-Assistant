# Client Data Residency and Handling Policy

Document ID: P01
Type: Policy
Owner: Delivery Risk & Compliance, Canada
Last updated: 2026-04-15
Status: Current

## Purpose
Sets where client data may be stored and processed on Canadian engagements.

## Rules
- Personal information of Canadian client end-users must be stored and processed in Canadian data centres (Canada Central or Canada East regions) unless the client signs a written residency exception.
- Non-production environments may use masked or synthetic data only. Real personal information in dev or test is not allowed.
- Cross-border access for support (for example, offshore teams) requires the client's written approval and is logged.
- Data residency commitments in a Statement of Work override this policy if they are stricter.

## Approvals
Residency exceptions are approved by the Delivery Risk & Compliance lead and recorded in the engagement's RAID log.

## Alignment
This policy follows PIPEDA principles and applicable provincial privacy laws, including Québec's Law 25.
