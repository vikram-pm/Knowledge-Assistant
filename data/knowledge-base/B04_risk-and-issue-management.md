# Risk and Issue Management

Document ID: B04
Type: Playbook
Owner: Delivery Excellence, Canada
Last updated: 2025-10-28
Status: Current

- Log every risk and issue in the RAID log (T05) within 1 business day of identification.
- Score risks on likelihood and impact (1–5 each). Risks scoring 15 or more are reviewed weekly with the client.
- Every risk needs an owner and a mitigation date.
- Close issues only when the client confirms resolution.
