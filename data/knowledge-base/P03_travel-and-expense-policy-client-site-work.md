# Travel and Expense Policy: Client Site Work

Document ID: P03
Type: Policy
Owner: Finance, Canada
Last updated: 2026-01-20
Status: Current

## Summary
Covers travel to client sites within Canada.

- Book flights and hotels through the corporate travel portal at least 7 days ahead.
- Hotel cap: $250 CAD per night in Toronto, Montréal and Vancouver; $190 CAD elsewhere.
- Meal per diem: $75 CAD per full travel day.
- Mileage for personal vehicle use: $0.70 per km.
- Submit expenses within 30 days with itemised receipts.
- Billable travel must be pre-approved by the delivery manager and match the client's SOW travel terms.
